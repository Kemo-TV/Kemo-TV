## About the M3U Playlist Inspector

The Inspector helps users review M3U and M3U8 playlist structure and metadata. Its local validator checks playlist formatting; it does **not** test whether stream URLs are online, playable, safe, or licensed.

## Before choosing a provider

Review the provider's current pricing, regional availability, supported devices, renewal and cancellation terms, privacy information, and authorisation to distribute the content offered in your location.

IPTV is a delivery technology, not a guarantee of content rights. Use services and access content only where you are legally entitled to do so.
